## 3. Historical Period Expansion

### Period Assignments Added

32 heritage entities were assigned to existing historical periods based on reasonable historical confidence:

**Ancient Period (-3300 to 700):**
- Ajanta Caves (2nd century BCE – 6th century CE)
- Sanchi Stupa (3rd century BCE)
- Kalaripayattu (ancient martial art)
- Theyyam (ancient ritual dance)
- Kite Festival / Uttarayan (ancient harvest festival)

**Chera/Chola Period (-300 to 1200):**
- Bharatanatyam (ancient classical dance, 2nd century CE)
- Chola Bronzes (Chola dynasty masterworks)
- Meenakshi Amman Temple (Pandya/Nayak period)

**Medieval Period (700 to 1300):**
- Khajuraho Temples (950–1050 CE)
- Konark Sun Temple (13th century)
- Pattachitra (centuries-old scroll painting)
- Phulkari (Punjab embroidery tradition)
- Gond Art (Madhya Pradesh tribal art)
- Bhangra (Punjab harvest dance)

**Ahom Period (1228 to 1826):**
- Majuli (river island with Ahom-era monasteries)
- Sattriya Dance (classical dance from Assam)
- Bhaona (Assamese dance-drama)
- Mask Making of Majuli (Satra craft tradition)
- Satras of Majuli (Vaishnavite monasteries)

**Sultanate Period (1300 to 1573):**
- Adalaj Stepwell (built 1498)
- Qutub Minar (built 1192–1368)
- Blue Pottery (introduced by Mughals)

**Sultanate/Kashmir Period (1300 to 1586):**
- Dard-Shina Culture (ancient Kashmir heritage)

**Colonial Period (1573 to 1947):**
- Amber Fort (built 1592)
- Hawa Mahal (built 1799)
- Red Fort (built 1638–1648)
- Golden Temple (founded 1577)
- Basilica of Bom Jesus (built 1594–1605)
- Sé Cathedral (built 1562–1640)
- Jallianwala Bagh (1919)
- Goa Carnival (Portuguese influence)
- Chandni Chowk (built 1650s)

**Kalinga Period (-261 to 1500):**
- Tribal Heritage of Odisha

**File:** `backend/src/routes/timeline.ts`

- **Vertical timeline line** with era-colored dots
- **Period cards** with gradient backgrounds, date ranges, and duration

  - Category badge
  - State/location context
- **Click-through navigation** to heritage detail pages
- **URL anchor support** for deep-linking to specific periods
- **Empty period handling** with helpful message
- **Responsive design** for mobile/tablet/desktop
